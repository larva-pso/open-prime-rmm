# Commercial use

OpenPrimeRMM is community-source/source-available. It is intended to let MSPs and internal IT teams self-host the platform for their own operations.

Permitted without a separate commercial license:

- An MSP self-hosts OpenPrimeRMM to manage its own customers.
- An MSP charges customers for managed IT, cybersecurity, helpdesk, patching, monitoring, backup, or support services.
- A company self-hosts OpenPrimeRMM to manage its own endpoints.
- Internal modifications for your own operations.

Not permitted without a separate commercial license:

- Selling OpenPrimeRMM itself.
- Charging specifically per seat, per device, per agent, per tenant, or per hosted OpenPrimeRMM instance.
- Offering OpenPrimeRMM as a hosted RMM/SaaS product to third parties.
- White-labeling or rebranding OpenPrimeRMM as your commercial RMM product.
- Bundling OpenPrimeRMM into a competing commercial endpoint-management platform.

Short version:

You can use it to run your MSP. You cannot sell OpenPrimeRMM as the thing being sold.
